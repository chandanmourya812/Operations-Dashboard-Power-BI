##  Project Overview

This project is an **interactive Operations Dashboard** built in Microsoft Power BI. It consolidates key business metrics — complaint resolution, attendance performance, and product-level KPIs — into a single, visual reporting interface.

The dashboard is designed for operations managers or business analysts who need a quick, clear overview of day-to-day business health.

---

##  Business Problem

Operations teams often work with data spread across multiple files — attendance sheets, complaint logs, payroll records. This dashboard brings it all together in one place and answers:

- How is our complaint resolution performance this month?
- Which product is generating the most support tickets?
- What is the overall employee attendance rate by department?
- Are we improving or declining compared to last month?

##  Tools & Features Used

- **Microsoft Power BI Desktop** *(free download)*
- Data import from Excel
- DAX measures — calculate KPIs like resolution rate, attendance %
- Bar Chart — complaints by product and month
- Pie Chart — issue type distribution
- KPI Cards — show headline numbers at a glance
- Slicers — filter by month, department, or product
- Drill-through — click any bar to see underlying records

---

##  Dashboard Sections

### Section 1 — Complaint Overview
- Total complaints registered
- Resolved vs Pending split
- Monthly trend line

### Section 2 — Product Performance
- Complaint volume by product (Face Reader / Fingerprint Scanner)
- Top issue types per product

### Section 3 — Attendance Summary
- Department-wise attendance rate
- Monthly attendance trend
- Late arrival tracking

##  Key Insights from the Dashboard

- Complaint resolution rate: **88%** this month
- Face Reader accounts for **58%** of all complaints
- Attendance rate across departments: **87%**
- Month-on-month improvement in resolution time: **↓ 0.8 days faster**
